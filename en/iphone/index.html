How to find your iPhone model
  1. Tap Settings.
  2. Tap General.
  3. Tap About.
  4. Model Name shows your iPhone model.
How to find your iOS version
  1. Tap Settings.
  2. Tap General.
  3. Tap About.
  4. The number next to iOS Version is your iOS version.
