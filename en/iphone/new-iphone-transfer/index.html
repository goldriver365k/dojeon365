Transfer to a new iPhone

These pictures are drawings of the iPhone screen. Menu names and positions may differ slightly from your iPhone.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Turn on the new iPhone.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Put it next to your old iPhone.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    On the old iPhone, tap Continue.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    Point the old iPhone’s camera at the blue pattern on the new one.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    Enter your old passcode on the new iPhone. Keep both close while transferring.
