Privacy Policy

DigiStep365 works without an account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

What is saved on your device

Your text size, your lesson position and the phone model you choose are saved only in this browser (local storage). They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Analytics

If you tap “Accept”, Google Analytics 4 counts page visits so we can see which lessons help. Nothing is loaded if you tap “No thanks”. You can change your choice below.

Share buttons

Share buttons open KakaoTalk, Facebook or WhatsApp only when you tap them. We do not send them any information; those services have their own privacy policies. The QR code and “Copy link” work inside your browser.

Links to other sites

Lessons link to Samsung’s official manuals and support pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
