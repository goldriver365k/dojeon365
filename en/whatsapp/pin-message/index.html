Pin a message in a group

These pictures are drawings of the WhatsApp screen. Menu names and positions may differ slightly from the real app.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    On the Chats screen, tap the chat with Family.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Touch and hold the message you want to pin.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    Tap the three dots at the top right.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    Tap “Pin”.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    The message stays at the top of the chat.
