Transfer chats from your old phone

These pictures are drawings of the WhatsApp screen. Menu names and positions may differ slightly from the real app.

  1. Step 1 of 4

    On the old phone, open Settings.

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Tap “Chats”.

  3. Step 3 of 4

    Tap “Transfer chats”.

  4. Step 4 of 4

    Tap “Start”. The new phone will show the next steps.

Chat backups can include private messages. Keep your Google account and backup password safe, and never delete a backup unless you are sure.
