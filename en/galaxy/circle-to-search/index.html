Search for things on your screen

Search for something on your screen by circling it with Circle to Search.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 3

    Touch and hold the Home button at the bottom of the screen.

  2. Step 2 of 3

    Draw a circle around the thing you want to know about.

  3. Step 3 of 3

    Read the search results at the bottom.

    Good to know Bixby Vision is available on the Galaxy S25, S24 and S23 series. The S26 uses this method (Circle to Search).
