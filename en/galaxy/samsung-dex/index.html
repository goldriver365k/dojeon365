Learn about Samsung DeX

Find out what Samsung DeX is and where its settings are.

  1. Step 1 of 4

    Tap the ‘Settings’ app.

    If it is not on the Home screen, swipe up to find it on the Apps screen.

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Tap ‘Connected devices’.

    If you cannot see it, swipe up to find it.

  3. Step 3 of 4

    Tap ‘Samsung DeX’.

    If you cannot see it, swipe up to find it.

  4. Step 4 of 4

    Read the information.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.
