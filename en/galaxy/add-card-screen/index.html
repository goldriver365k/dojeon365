Look at the add card screen

Look at the screen for adding a payment card to Samsung Wallet.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Tap the ‘Samsung Wallet’ app.

    If it is not on the Home screen, swipe up to find it on the Apps screen.

    Good to know This lesson changes real settings or has real results. Read all the steps first, then do them on your phone.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Tap ‘+’ (Add) at the top.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    Look at what you can add.

    You can add payment cards, transit cards and more, depending on your country.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    Tap ‘Payment card’.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    Read the instructions for adding a card.
