Use mobile hotspot

Share your phone’s internet connection with another device using Mobile Hotspot.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 6

    Tap the ‘Settings’ app.

    If it is not on the Home screen, swipe up to find it on the Apps screen.

  2. Step 2 of 6

    Tap ‘Connections’.

    If you cannot see it, swipe up to find it.

  3. Step 3 of 6

    Tap ‘Mobile Hotspot and Tethering’.

    If you cannot see it, swipe up to find it.

  4. Step 4 of 6

    Tap ‘Mobile Hotspot’.

    If you cannot see it, swipe up to find it.

  5. Step 5 of 6

    Tap the ‘Mobile Hotspot’ switch to turn it on.

    Good to know This may cost extra. Check your mobile plan first.

  6. Step 6 of 6

    On the other device, choose your phone’s name from its Wi-Fi list.
