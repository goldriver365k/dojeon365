Turn on the speaker during a call

Turn on the speaker during a call so you can talk without holding the phone to your ear.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 3

    Make or answer a call to start talking.

  2. Step 2 of 3

    Tap ‘Speaker’.

    You can hear and talk with the phone away from your ear.

  3. Step 3 of 3

    To turn the speaker off, tap ‘Speaker’ again.
