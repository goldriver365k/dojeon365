When your phone gets hot

What to do when your phone gets hot.

  1. Step 1 of 4

    If it is charging, unplug the charger.

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Tap the Recents button.

  3. Step 3 of 4

    Tap ‘Close all’ to close the apps.

  4. Step 4 of 4

    Stop using the phone for a while and let it cool in a cool place.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.
