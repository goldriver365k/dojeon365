Send a video to family

Send a video from Gallery to a family member with the Messages app.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

New lesson written for the global site. Screens of Google apps change often, so check on a real phone.

  1. Step 1 of 6

    Tap the ‘Gallery’ app.

    If it is not on the Home screen, swipe up to find it on the Apps screen.

  2. Step 2 of 6

    Tap the video you want to send.

  3. Step 3 of 6

    Tap ‘Share’ at the bottom.

  4. Step 4 of 6

    Tap ‘Messages’.

  5. Step 5 of 6

    Choose the person to send it to.

  6. Step 6 of 6

    Tap the Send button.

    Good to know Long videos may be too big for a text message. Then use Quick Share or email instead.
