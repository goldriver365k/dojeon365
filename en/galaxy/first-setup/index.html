Set up your phone for the first time

Set up a new Galaxy phone for the first time.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 4

    Press and hold the Side button to turn the phone on.

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Check your language and tap ‘Start’.

  3. Step 3 of 4

    Choose a Wi-Fi network and connect.

  4. Step 4 of 4

    For the rest, follow the instructions on the screen and tap ‘Next’.
