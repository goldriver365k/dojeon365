Fix typing mistakes

Fix typing mistakes with the delete key and by moving the cursor.

  1. Step 1 of 3

    Tap the delete key (⌫) once.

    The letter just before the cursor is deleted.

  2. Step 2 of 3

    Type the correct letter.

  3. Step 3 of 3

    To fix a letter in the middle, tap that spot.

    The cursor (the blinking line) moves to where you tapped.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.
