Make folders and widgets

Group apps into a folder and add a weather widget to the Home screen.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 6

    Touch and hold an app on the Home screen and drag it onto another app.

    A folder with both apps is created.

  2. Step 2 of 6

    Tap the folder name and type a name.

  3. Step 3 of 6

    Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home screen.

  4. Step 4 of 6

    Tap ‘Widgets’.

  5. Step 5 of 6

    Tap ‘Weather’.

  6. Step 6 of 6

    Tap ‘Add’.
