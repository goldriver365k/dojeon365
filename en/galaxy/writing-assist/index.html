Polish and summarize text

Fix spelling and grammar in a message with the Galaxy AI keyboard tool.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Tap the ‘Messages’ app.

    If it is not on the Home screen, swipe up to find it on the Apps screen.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Tap a conversation.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    Type your message.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    Tap the Galaxy AI icon at the top left of the keyboard.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    Tap ‘Spelling and grammar’.

    It fixes mistakes. ‘Summarize’ makes long text shorter.
