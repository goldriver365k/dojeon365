When the screen freezes

Force a frozen Galaxy phone to restart with two buttons.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 2

    Press and hold the Side button and the Volume down button together for more than 7 seconds.

    Keep holding until the screen turns off.

  2. Step 2 of 2

    When the screen turns off, let go and wait.
