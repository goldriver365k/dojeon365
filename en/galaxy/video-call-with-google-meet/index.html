Make a video call with Google Meet

Make a video call to family with the Google Meet app.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

New lesson written for the global site. Screens of Google apps change often, so check on a real phone.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Tap the ‘Meet’ app.

    If it is not on the Home screen, swipe up to find it on the Apps screen.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Tap ‘New’.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    Tap the name of the person to call.

    They also need the Google Meet app or a Google account.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    Tap the video call button.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    When you finish, tap the red End call button.

Well done! You finished this lesson.
