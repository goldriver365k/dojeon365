Email photos with Gmail

Send photos to family by email with the Gmail app on a Galaxy phone.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

New lesson written for the global site. Screens of Google apps change often, so check on a real phone.

  1. Step 1 of 7

    Tap the ‘Gmail’ app.

    If it is not on the Home screen, swipe up to find it on the Apps screen.

  2. Step 2 of 7

    Tap ‘Compose’ at the bottom right.

  3. Step 3 of 7

    Type the email address of the person in the ‘To’ box.

  4. Step 4 of 7

    Tap the paper clip (Attach) at the top.

  5. Step 5 of 7

    Tap the photo you want to send.

    You can tap more than one photo.

  6. Step 6 of 7

    Type a short subject.

  7. Step 7 of 7

    Tap the Send button (paper plane) at the top.
