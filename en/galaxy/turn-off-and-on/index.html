Turn your phone off and on again

Turn a Galaxy phone off with the Side and Volume down buttons, then turn it back on.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 4

    Press and hold the Side button and the Volume down button together.

    Press both at the same time and wait for the menu to appear.

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Tap Power off.

  3. Step 3 of 4

    Tap Power off once more.

    When the screen goes dark, go to the next step to turn it on again.

  4. Step 4 of 4

    Press and hold the Side button to turn the phone on.

    Hold it for a few seconds until the Samsung logo appears. Then unlock the screen.
