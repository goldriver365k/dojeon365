Charge your phone

Charge your Galaxy phone step by step: plug in the charger, connect the cable and check the charging sign.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.

  1. Step 1 of 4

    Plug the charger into a wall outlet.

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Plug the end of the cable into the port at the bottom of your phone.

    If the port is wet, let it dry completely first.

  3. Step 3 of 4

    Check that a charging sign appears on the screen.

  4. Step 4 of 4

    Unplug the cable when charging is finished.
