Lock and unlock the screen

Lock the screen with the Side button and unlock it again by swiping up.

  1. Step 1 of 3

    Press the Side button once, briefly.

    The screen turns off and locks automatically.

  2. Step 2 of 3

    Press the Side button briefly again.

    The lock screen turns on. You can also double-tap the screen.

  3. Step 3 of 3

    Swipe up on the screen.

    If you set a PIN or fingerprint, use it to unlock.

Written for Galaxy S26 · One UI 9 · Android 17. Menu names can differ on other models and versions.
